# PseudoTV Live

Turn your Kodi library into a live TV experience.

## What is PseudoTV Live?

PseudoTV Live transforms your Kodi library content into continuous, linear TV channels. Movies, shows, and live content flow together as real TV — complete with scheduled programming, channel surfing, and an electronic program guide (EPG).

No tuner needed. No cable bill. Just your library, playing like TV.

## How it works

PseudoTV Live integrates with two key Kodi components:

- **PVR Backend** — PseudoTV acts as a virtual PVR server, creating logical TV channels from your library content
- **IPTV Simple Client** — Delivers the channel data and stream to Kodi's live TV interface

The result: your content appears in Kodi's native TV section, indistinguishable from a real TV source.

## Requirements

- **Kodi 19+** (Matrix or later)
- **IPTV Simple Client** PVR addon
- A library with video content (movies, TV shows)

## Quick Start

1. Install IPTV Simple Client from the Kodi addon repository
2. Install PseudoTV Live
3. Configure your library sources in Kodi settings
4. Open PseudoTV Live and generate your channels
5. Start Live TV in Kodi — your content is now channelized

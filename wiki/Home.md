# Home

**PseudoTV Live** turns your Kodi library into an always-on set of live TV channels. It builds a virtual channel lineup from the movies, shows and music you already own, keeps a rolling electronic programme guide (EPG) for them, and serves it to Kodi through IPTV Simple — so you can browse, flip and watch your own content like broadcast television.

## The short version

1. **Autotune** builds a starting lineup from your video library, or open the **Channel Manager** and build one by hand.
2. Rules shape each channel — sort order, duration, fillers, filters, scheduling and playback behaviour.
3. PseudoTV keeps building the guide in the background and serves it to IPTV Simple.
4. Flip channels in Kodi, or manage everything from a browser with the **Web Channel Manager**.

Two levels of configuration apply to every channel: **global** defaults set on the Settings page, and **per-channel** rules set in either manager. The per-channel rule always wins. That distinction is explained on [Settings](Settings) and in full on [Rules](Rules).
